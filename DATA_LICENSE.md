# Data license

Project-generated images captured with the M5Stack CoreS3 and their author-created annotations in `data/cores3_adaptation`, `data/cores3_frozen_validation`, and `data/on_device_evidence` are released under **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)** by the authors of the associated paper.

This statement does **not** apply to third-party datasets, Ultralytics/Espressif software, or other dependencies. External imagery with undocumented redistribution terms is not included in this public-ready package.
